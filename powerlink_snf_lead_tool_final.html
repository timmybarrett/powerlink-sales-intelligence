Status changes are saved in this browser only. To carry them into next month's refresh: export (no filters applied), save the file as status_import.csv next to refresh_hospital_hot_leads.py, then re-run the script -- it will read the statuses back into the ledger automatically. CCN = CMS Certification Number. "Condition-level" is the hospital equivalent of a nursing home's actual-harm/immediate-jeopardy tier; "Complaint-triggered" means the survey was launched because someone reported a problem, even where the finding landed at the Standard tier.

"Send to CRM" pushes the lead to SharpSpring (create-or-update) with the selected rep as owner, via the same n8n integration used by the SNF tool.
