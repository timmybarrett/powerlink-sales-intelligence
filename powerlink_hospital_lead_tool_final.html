Status changes are saved in this browser only. CCN = CMS Certification Number. "Condition-level" is the actual-harm/immediate-jeopardy tier; "Complaint-triggered" means the survey was launched because someone reported a problem, even where the finding landed at the Standard tier.

"Send to CRM" pushes the lead to SharpSpring (create-or-update) with the selected rep as owner, via the same n8n integration used by both tools.
